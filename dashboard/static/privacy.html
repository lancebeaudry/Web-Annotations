Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

This policy explains what PinPoint, operated by Avalanche Creative, collects, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it short on purpose; if anything is unclear, email projects@avalanchegr.com.

What we collect

Account holders. Your email address, the sign-in codes we send to it, the projects you create (site name and address, project token), the collaborators you invite (their email addresses) and the notification lists you set. If you upgrade to a paid plan, Stripe collects your payment details; we receive only a customer reference, your subscription status and the billing email.

Reviewers. When you leave feedback, we store the comment, the name you entered, the page and element it was pinned to (including a snippet of that element's text and styles), your browser and screen size, recent console errors from that page, an automatic screenshot of the area around the element (project owners can turn this off), any image you attach, and the time. Signed-in reviewers are identified by email; guests on open-feedback projects are identified only by the name they type and a random session identifier stored in their browser.

Everyone. Standard server logs (IP address, browser, time of request) kept by our hosting providers for security and troubleshooting, and the technical context a comment needs to be useful to a developer.

We do not run advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent personal data.

How we use it

To run the service: showing feedback on the right site to the right people, sending sign-in codes, sending notification emails to the people a project owner has chosen, and sending mention alerts when a reviewer tags a teammate. To bill paid subscriptions. To provide support and investigate abuse. To send occasional product updates to account owners, which you can opt out of from any such email.

Support access

A small number of Avalanche Creative staff, called operators, can view and moderate any project. They use that access only to provide support, resolve technical problems and enforce our terms. We do not browse customer projects otherwise.

Where your data lives and who processes it

PinPoint runs on the following providers, each of which processes data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and server functions), hosted in the United States.
  • Stripe for payments. Card numbers go directly to Stripe and never touch our systems.
  • Resend for sending sign-in codes and notification emails.
  • GitHub Pages for serving the dashboard and this website.
  • Slack and ClickUp, only if a project owner connects them: comment text, page links and status changes are sent to the channel or list they chose.

Feedback you leave on a customer's site is also visible to that customer and to the collaborators and guests they allow. Each project is isolated; reviewers only ever see the project whose link they hold.

Cookies and browser storage

PinPoint uses your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies, to keep you signed in, remember the name you entered as a reviewer, and remember small preferences such as whether resolved pins are hidden. Clearing your browser storage signs you out and forgets those preferences.

Retention and deletion

Feedback stays until the project owner deletes the comment or the project. Deleting a comment removes its attached images; deleting a project removes all of its comments and images. Image files that are no longer referenced by any comment are removed by an hourly cleanup. Closing your account deletes your projects and their data. Billing records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your rights

You can see and export your project's feedback from the dashboard and the overlay at any time. Account owners can delete their projects and account themselves. If you are a reviewer who wants a comment removed, ask the project owner, or email us and we will help. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including access, correction, deletion and portability; email us to exercise them and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

PinPoint is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to projects is enforced in the database for every request, not just in the interface. Sign-in uses one-time codes, so there is no password for us to store or for anyone to steal. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you promptly.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced by email to account owners or in the dashboard before they take effect.
