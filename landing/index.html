The short answers.

Do my clients need an account?+
No. With open feedback on, anyone with your link types their name and starts commenting. To control access, invite specific emails and they sign in with a one-time code. There's still no password.
Will visitors see anything on my site?+
Never. The feedback layer only loads for visits that include your private ?markup= link. Everyone else sees your site as usual.
Does it work outside WordPress?+
Yes. The WordPress plugin is the easiest install, but the same one-line script works on Webflow, Squarespace, Shopify, static sites and custom apps.
Will it slow my site down or change how it looks?+
No. The script does nothing unless the PinPoint link is used, and the interface runs in its own isolated layer, so your styles and the tool never collide.
Where does the feedback live?+
In our hosted backend, isolated per project. Only the owner, invited collaborators and, on open-feedback sites, people with the link can see it. Avalanche staff can access projects for support, as disclosed in our privacy policy.
What happens if I cancel?+
Your oldest project keeps working on the free plan and the rest go read-only. Nothing is deleted, and re-subscribing unfreezes them instantly.
Can my AI coding assistant work the list?+
Yes. Every export includes item IDs, a project key and the exact commands, and PinPoint runs a hosted MCP server so Claude Code can see feedback as tools: list items, triage, reply, set a status, assign. No install, one command to connect. Replies are signed as a person on your team unless you choose to label them as an AI assistant.
What does triage look like?+
Each item can carry labels (bug, copy, design, content needed, photo needed, decision), an effort size (quick, medium, large) and one of five statuses, including "Waiting on client". The inbox shows the counts up top: what's waiting on the client, quick wins, medium, large, untriaged. Your AI assistant can do the first pass, and the "Needs your decision" email sends the client only the items that are theirs.
What is a page approval?+
On the Agency plan, an owner or invited collaborator can approve a page. It records who signed off and when, and turns commenting off on that page until someone reopens it. Replies on existing threads still work.
What counts toward the free plan's 50 comments?+
Every comment and reply on the project. When you reach it the project goes read-only for new comments until you upgrade or delete some. Automatic screenshots count toward the 10-image allowance; you can turn them off per project.
Who makes this?+
