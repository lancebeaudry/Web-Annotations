A good fit

  • You're willing to follow a short checklist, even if you've never used a coding assistant

Not a fit, yet

Questions people ask first.

Do I need to be technical?+

No. If you can follow a one-page checklist, you can run this. You will open one application and paste one instruction. We practice it together until it's boring.

Which AI assistant do you set up?+

Claude Code or ChatGPT Codex. Both can read PinPoint requests and edit a site. If you already pay for one, we use that. If you don't, we'll help you pick on the fit call.

What if I don't have a staging site?+

We recommend every change happens on a staging copy first. Many hosts offer one at no cost, and turning it on is part of the setup. If yours doesn't, we'll tell you on the fit call what your options are before you pay anything.

Does my site need to be in GitHub?+

No. A repository makes undo easier and we'll set one up if you want it, in your name. Without one, your host's backups are the way back, and we make sure they work before we hand over.

What happens if the AI makes a bad change?+

It happens on staging, where nobody sees it, and you can undo it. We show you how before we finish. After the setup, reviewing and publishing changes is yours. If you would rather have us on call for that, ask about hosting and support.

Can you just make the changes for me?+

Yes. We offer hosting and support where you make requests in PinPoint and we get them live. Tell us on the fit call and we'll skip straight to that.

What should I have ready for the call?+

Your site's address, who hosts it, whether you have the login, and a rough idea of what you change most often. That's enough for us to tell you whether it's a fit.
